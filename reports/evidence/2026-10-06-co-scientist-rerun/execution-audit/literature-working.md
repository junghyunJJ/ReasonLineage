# Rerun scientific audit working notes

Audit uses primary publications and official labels after generation; no feedback is passed to generators. This is claim checking, not biological validation or an exhaustive literature review.

## ALS

Initial four multi-model drafts all nominate NUP98. The initial seed pool is therefore mechanistically narrow. All call it a hypothesis; [S1]-[S4] do not establish an initiating neuronal nucleoporin-phosphorylation event.

[Laurell et al., Cell 2011; PMID21335236](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21335236/) supports multisite NUP98 phosphorylation by CDK1/NEKs in mitotic NPC disassembly. Its setting does not directly validate CDK5, p38/MK2, postmitotic ALS motor neurons, or the proposed chronological chain. The neuronal extension remains an inference to test.

[Fontoura et al., J Cell Biol1999; PMID10087256](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10087256/) and [Rosenblum/Blobel1999; PMID10500183](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10500183/) establish precursor processing/autoproteolysis in NUP98/NUP96 biogenesis. Audit endogenous replacements for processing, abundance and localization controls. A mutation's transport effect alone does not uniquely identify phosphorylation causality; this is an audit inference from these findings.

## AML

Pending completed new outputs. The frozen S4 DHODH link is already established. An approved drug nomination needs indication, exposure, normal-cell effects and prior AML literature checked separately. Novel context/refutation strategy must be distinguished from a new target discovery.

AML primary records: [Biswas et al.,2025 PMID41061202](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41061202/) already evaluates teriflunomide for HMA-resistant TP53-mutated AML, including terminal differentiation and schedule-dependent pyrimidine salvage. This limits an assertion that the drug-DHODH-AML differentiation link is new; different residual-cell context remains a separate unvalidated claim. [PMID31856268](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31856268/) examines atovaquone in AML. [2023 PMID36831684](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36831684/) reports pediatric AML combination feasibility and associated model/correlative work; it does not validate the proposed salvage-low cytarabine-residual differentiation chain. [EMA Arava](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/arava) lists active rheumatoid and psoriatic arthritis in adults; FDA indication should be described separately, not treated as an identical global label.

Official indication: [DailyMed MEPRON current label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=b426b6bf-f07e-4580-97ae-dfca1ddf5b8f) confirms atovaquone suspension for prevention/treatment of Pneumocystis pneumonia in specified populations, not AML approval. Combination malaria indications must not be assigned automatically to standalone MEPRON. Current hypothesis labels indication/exposure recollections as unverified prior knowledge, rather than claiming observed AML clinical benefit.
