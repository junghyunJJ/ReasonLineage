- Packet S1 supports TDP-43-related pore/transport disruption in the investigated systems, not an initiating NUP98 phosphorylation mechanism: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29311743/ .
- NUP98 phosphorylation promotes pore disassembly during mitotic entry with CDK1/Nek involvement. That context is dividing cells, not proof of a CDK5 mechanism in ALS motor neurons: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21335236/ (Cell 2011; DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2011.01.012).

This supports neuronal kinase/nuclear-envelope relevance, not direct NUP98 phosphorylation or the proposed TARDBP-specific causal order: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21389115/ (DOI 10.1091/mbc.E10-07-0654).
- GPT ALS initial c002 explicitly labels CDK5→NUP98 and causal progression speculative; these links remain unverified hypotheses, not established facts.

## AML preliminary checks

- GPT AML drafts use teriflunomide, a drug whose MS indication, DHODH activity and >99% plasma protein binding can be checked in the FDA label: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/202992s018lbl.pdf . FDA 2026 label sections 1, 12.1 and 12.3 confirm relapsing forms of MS in adults, DHODH inhibition and >99% binding. Do not infer achievable unbound AML exposure from total plasma levels.
- Original AML DHODH/differentiation evidence is packet S4: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27641501/ . This does not establish selective teriflunomide efficacy in the proposed cytarabine-persistent, salvage-limited context.
- Existing teriflunomide AML work includes TP53-mutated/HMA-resistant cells, terminal differentiation and compensatory salvage effects: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41061202/ . Merely naming teriflunomide for AML is not a new discovery; any novelty claim must specify and verify the new context/prediction.
