# P67a891ee11

Phosphorylation of Nup98 at proline-directed sites amplifies nuclear-import failure after cytoplasmic TDP-43 assembly

This hypothesis is restricted to experimental TDP-43 proteinopathy. Persistent cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies are the initiating lesion in this setting. Phosphorylation of the FG-nucleoporin Nup98 at proline-directed sites is proposed as the next, pore-intrinsic step that impairs nuclear import. Loss of nuclear TDP-43 and later cell death are downstream responses. The same order is not proposed for every sporadic or C9orf72-associated ALS trajectory.

Supported premises are limited to the supplied studies. Nuclear-pore and transport components associate with insoluble TDP-43, and disease-linked TDP-43 disrupts nucleoporin localization and nuclear transport in experimental cells, with related pore pathology in ALS tissue [S1]. Cytoplasmic TDP-43 can de-mix without conventional stress granules, accumulate in a phosphorylated form, mislocalize transport-associated proteins, and accompany import failure, nuclear TDP-43 loss, and cell death [S3]. Phosphorylation of TDP-43 in those assemblies is a modification of TDP-43, not evidence of nucleoporin phosphorylation [S3]. Expanded C9orf72 repeat RNA is linked to RanGAP mislocalization and impaired nuclear import in models and patient-derived neurons [S2]. Cellular stress can recruit transport factors into stress granules and impair transport, and altering stress-granule assembly changes that phenotype [S4]. Placed side by side, [S3] and [S4] support two routes to transport failure: a stress-granule-independent TDP-43-assembly route and a sequestration route.

Speculative bridges are not established by [S1]–[S4]. The substrate is assumed to be Nup98. Unverified prior knowledge: Nup98 is an FG-nucleoporin, and phosphorylation of FG-nucleoporins in mitotic systems accompanies loss of selective nucleocytoplasmic transport. A further assumption is that a proline-directed kinase induced after cytoplasmic TDP-43 assembly phosphorylates CDK-consensus sites in the Nup98 FG region in post-mitotic neurons and thereby weakens FG–karyopherin interactions. No supplied source names that kinase, those residues, or that interaction.

The causal order under test is cytoplasmic TDP-43 assembly, then Nup98 phosphorylation, then import failure, then nuclear TDP-43 depletion and death. Stress-granule sequestration of transport factors [S4] and repeat-RNA-associated RanGAP displacement [S2] are alternative routes. A third alternative is direct capture of nucleoporins and transport factors by TDP-43 assemblies, so the import defect associated with [S1] and [S3] proceeds while Nup98 remains unphosphorylated. A fourth alternative is that Nup98 phosphorylation is only a late correlate of cellular injury.

The discriminating preclinical test uses inducible neurons that form persistent cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies, with a parallel stress that forms stress granules without those assemblies. The time series records assembly markers, stress-granule markers, Nup98 phosphorylation at the prespecified sites, Nup98 localization, a quantitative nuclear-import reporter, nuclear TDP-43, and death. Endogenous Nup98 is replaced by a phospho-deficient or phosphomimetic mutant confined to the assumed sites, and a kinase inhibitor is added only after assemblies are present. Concordant support requires four results: phospho-deficient Nup98 preserves import and nuclear TDP-43 while assemblies persist; the phosphomimetic slows import in cells that lack TDP-43 assemblies; post-assembly kinase inhibition lowers Nup98 phosphorylation and restores import; and blocking stress granules leaves both Nup98 phosphorylation and the import defect intact in the TDP-43 arm. A C9orf72 repeat-RNA culture is a boundary condition. Unchanged Nup98 phosphorylation there would show that the RanGAP-linked route in [S2] can impair import without this Nup98 step.

A falsifying observation in the TDP-43 model is absence of a rise in site-specific Nup98 phosphorylation before import fails, an unchanged course of import failure, nuclear TDP-43 loss, and death in the phospho-deficient mutant despite continued assembly formation, or intact import in cells that express the phosphomimetic and lack TDP-43 pathology. Phosphorylation is reclassified as a downstream response if it appears only after import has already failed and delayed kinase inhibition leaves import unchanged.

Limitations follow from the evidence boundaries. Kinase identity, residue identity, and the choice of Nup98 are assumptions, and amino-acid substitutions can disturb pore structure independently of phosphate. Acute experimental timing does not establish the sequence in human sporadic tissue or in every C9orf72 case [S1], [S2]. Rescue limited to one reporter would narrow the claim to that cargo. The design tests a mechanism and does not validate a treatment.
