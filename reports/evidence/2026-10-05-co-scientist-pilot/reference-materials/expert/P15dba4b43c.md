# P15dba4b43c

Improved research hypothesis

Title: Premature stress-kinase phosphorylation of Nup98 initiates nucleocytoplasmic transport failure upstream of TDP-43 mislocalization in ALS motor neurons.

Hypothesis and causal chain. In TDP-43-positive ALS motor neurons — C9orf72 expansion carriers first, with non-C9 sporadic disease treated as an open subgroup — an aberrantly activated, proline-directed stress kinase phosphorylates nucleoporin Nup98 at sites homologous to those used during mitotic pore disassembly. Premature phosphorylation weakens Nup98 association with the pore scaffold, yielding a measurable pore-composition signature (loss of retention of other nucleoporins) that precedes Ran-dependent import failure, nuclear TDP-43 depletion, cytoplasmic TDP-43 de-mixing, and cell death. Phosphorylation is the initiating event; TDP-43 pathology and transport-factor sequestration are downstream responses.

Supported premises. (1) TDP-43 pathology is physically linked to nuclear-pore/transport components, with pore abnormalities in ALS tissue [S1]. (2) C9orf72 expansion disrupts import and RanGAP localization, and transport manipulations modify phenotypes [S2], making transport failure a plausible disease node. (3) Persistent cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies impair import, deplete nuclear TDP-43, and cause death independently of stress granules [S3]. (4) Stress granules can sequester transport factors and impair transport [S4], defining the main alternative. No supplied source establishes a nucleoporin phosphorylation initiator.

Speculative bridges, explicitly gated (assumptions). That Nup98 carries mitotic-disassembly phosphosites and that stress kinases can re-phosphorylate it in post-mitotic neurons is unverified prior knowledge; the sites and kinase are unknown. Therefore every perturbational arm is gated on a preliminary phosphoproteomic mapping result (Arm 0). Because the initiating step is load-bearing, no phosphorylation, site, or kinase claim is treated as evidence. The precommitted default kinase candidate is a Cdk-family kinase (e.g., Cdk5, a neuronal proline-directed kinase); this candidate, its site targeting, and inhibitor specificity are unverified prior knowledge and serve only as an initial hypothesis if the mapped motif is S/TP.

Alternative explanations. (a) Sequestration-first: stress granules sequester Ran/import factors with no pore modification [S4]. (b) TDP-43-first: cytoplasmic assemblies physically displace pore components, with phosphorylation secondary [S1, S3]. (c) Generic collapse: broad energetic/proteostatic stress with nonspecific phosphorylation.

Discriminating strategy. Systems: iPSC-derived motor neurons from C9orf72 ALS patients and non-C9 sporadic ALS patients with TDP-43 pathology, isogenic CRISPR-corrected controls, and an acute stress model.

Arm 0 (gating and specificity): time-resolved phosphoproteomics of enriched pore fractions with phospho-specific antibodies to mapped Nup98 sites, tracked in parallel with live NLS-GFP import kinetics, RanGAP localization, and endogenous TDP-43 localization. Simultaneously record global-stress readouts (stress/translation markers and a panel of unrelated phosphosites) to show Nup98 phosphorylation is specific, not a bystander of cellular collapse, excluding alternative (c). Prediction: Nup98 phosphorylation rises before import decline and nuclear TDP-43 loss. All later arms proceed only if this gate passes.

Arm 1 (necessity, deconfounded editing): inducible, post-differentiation knock-in of non-phosphorylatable Nup98 (S-to-A) at mapped sites in already post-mitotic motor neurons, avoiding pre-mitotic editing that could impair mitotic pore disassembly, cell fitness, and cloning. Controls: cell fitness and NPC composition. Prediction: rescued import and reduced TDP-43 mislocalization despite repeats or stress.

Arm 2 (sufficiency, not mimic-dependent): S-to-D/E knock-in is included but treated as a weak mimic that may fail structurally. Orthogonal sufficiency: inducible recruitment of the candidate kinase (or inducible phosphatase interference) to the nuclear pore in healthy control neurons. Prediction: either manipulation alone induces import failure and nuclear TDP-43 depletion without stress or pre-existing aggregation. A negative phosphomimetic alone would not falsify the hypothesis.

Arm 3 (conditional staged inhibition): only after Arm 0 fixes one kinase-inhibitor pair via mapped phosphosite motif and in vitro kinase screening, apply early versus late inhibition, paired with genetic tools (kinase knockdown/kinase-dead) and off-target controls. Prediction: early inhibition rescues transport and prevents TDP-43 mislocalization; late inhibition after de-mixing does not.

Arm 4 (sequestration discrimination, [S4]): repeat assays in stress-granule-blocked neurons (e.g., G3BP1/2 loss; perturbation rationale unverified prior knowledge), plus a head-to-head comparison of transport signatures — pore-composition imaging of other Nups' retention and cargo-selective import assays — to separate scaffold weakening from import-factor sequestration. Prediction: persistent import impairment with a pore-composition signature under Nup98 phosphorylation; pure sequestration predicts rescue with preserved pore composition.

Arm 5 (TDP-43-first discrimination, [S1, S3]): block cytoplasmic assembly using temporally controlled aggregation-defective TDP-43 variants (unverified prior knowledge), not nuclear-restricted TDP-43, which itself alters RNA metabolism and would confound the test. Prediction: early Nup98 phosphorylation and import loss persist even when cytoplasmic assembly is blocked.

Falsifying observation. The hypothesis is falsified if import failure and nuclear TDP-43 loss precede detectable Nup98 phosphorylation in time-resolved assays; if post-mitotic non-phosphorylatable knock-in fails to rescue while stress-granule disruption rescues; or if the orthogonal pore-targeted kinase/phosphatase-arm fails to impair transport. Any such outcome demotes phosphorylation to a downstream marker at best.

Limitations. Phosphosite detection in scarce motor-neuron material may lack depth; if Arm 0 fails, the hypothesis stalls untested at the perturbational level; iPSC neurons do not reproduce decades of aging; kinase selection could be wrong despite motif screening; cell-level rescue demonstrates necessity and sufficiency only in vitro, not initiation in patients; subtype heterogeneity is unresolved.
