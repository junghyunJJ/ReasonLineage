# c001

Hypothesis title: Phosphorylation of channel FG-nucleoporins initiates import failure and nuclear TDP-43 loss in TDP-43 proteinopathy ALS

This hypothesis applies to TDP-43 proteinopathy, the setting described for experimental TDP-43 models and related ALS tissue [S1], [S3]. It leaves C9orf72 repeat disease on a separate path, in which expanded repeat RNA is linked to RanGAP mislocalization and impaired nuclear import [S2].

Supported premises. Insoluble TDP-43 aggregates associate with nuclear-pore and transport components; disease-linked TDP-43 disrupts nucleoporin localization and nuclear transport in cell systems, and related pore pathology is present in ALS tissue [S1]. Persistent cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies can form without conventional stress granules, mislocalize transport-associated proteins, and accompany impaired transport, loss of nuclear TDP-43, and cell death [S3]. Phosphorylated TDP-43 accumulates in those assemblies [S3]; that finding concerns TDP-43 and is not evidence of nucleoporin phosphorylation. Cellular stress can also recruit transport factors into stress granules and impair transport, and changing stress-granule assembly can modify those defects [S4]. Transport-directed interventions can modify C9orf72-model phenotypes [S2]. Across [S1]–[S4], no source identifies nucleoporin phosphorylation as an initiating ALS cause.

Speculative bridges, stated as assumptions. The proposed initiating event is phosphorylation of phenylalanine-glycine nucleoporins that line the central channel, provisionally Nup98 and Nup62. Unverified prior knowledge holds that proline-directed kinases phosphorylate FG-nucleoporins during mitosis and thereby weaken FG cohesion and barrier function. It is an assumption, unsupported by the packet, that a stress-activated proline-directed kinase modifies Nup98 and Nup62 at analogous sites on assembled pores in post-mitotic motor neurons, and that this change slows facilitated import while the pore scaffold remains in place. Further assumptions are that reduced TDP-43 import follows that barrier change and precedes large cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies, and that the relevant phospho-species first reside on NPC-associated nucleoporins.

Causal chain. Kinase-dependent FG-nucleoporin phosphorylation on assembled pores is the initiating mechanism. Impaired selective import, progressive loss of nuclear TDP-43, expansion of cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies, and later neuronal death are downstream responses. Sequestration of transport factors in stress granules [S4], capture of transport proteins by stress-granule-independent TDP-43 assemblies [S3], and co-aggregation of nucleoporins with insoluble TDP-43 [S1] can amplify injury after the barrier has changed. C9orf72-linked RanGAP mislocalization [S2] remains a genotype-specific route outside this initiating step.

Alternatives to be discriminated. Transport factors may be sequestered into stress granules during stress, impairing import without covalent nucleoporin modification [S4]. Cytoplasmic TDP-43 de-mixing may capture transport proteins and deplete nuclear TDP-43 without conventional stress granules [S3]. Nucleoporin mislocalization may arise only as a consequence of TDP-43 aggregation [S1]. FG-nucleoporin phosphorylation may appear late, after import has already failed, as a byproduct of broad kinase activation. In C9orf72 disease, repeat RNA and RanGAP pathology may initiate transport failure with no requirement for FG-nucleoporin phosphorylation [S2].

Discriminating preclinical test. In human iPSC-derived motor neurons with inducible cytoplasmic-biased TDP-43, and in matched C9orf72-repeat and isogenic control cultures, collect time-resolved measurements of Nup98 and Nup62 phosphorylation by mass spectrometry and site-specific detection, fractionation of those phospho-species into NPC-enriched versus stress-granule and insoluble TDP-43 material, a quantitative nuclear-import reporter, nuclear versus cytoplasmic TDP-43, G3BP1-positive stress granules, and a death marker. Replace the assumed phospho-acceptor residues with non-phosphorylatable and phospho-mimetic alleles at endogenous expression. Suppress stress-granule assembly by G3BP disruption so the sequestration route in [S4] is limited while pore phosphorylation remains measurable. Inhibit the assumed proline-directed kinase in two windows: before import decline, and only after nuclear TDP-43 has fallen. The initiating-phosphorylation account predicts NPC-enriched phospho-FG-nucleoporin before nuclear TDP-43 loss; import failure from the phospho-mimetic allele in the absence of stress granules; preserved import with the non-phosphorylatable allele despite cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies; prevention of both the phospho-mark and the import defect by early kinase inhibition; and persistence of an already established import defect when inhibition begins late. A C9orf72 culture in which RanGAP mislocalization and import failure proceed without the same NPC phospho-mark would keep the claim inside TDP-43 proteinopathy.

Falsifying observation. The hypothesis is false in this TDP-43 model if facilitated import and nuclear TDP-43 decline without an antecedent increase in Nup98 or Nup62 phosphorylation at the tested sites, or if non-phosphorylatable FG-nucleoporins leave import unchanged and phospho-mimetic FG-nucleoporins leave import unchanged once stress-granule assembly and TDP-43 solubility are matched. A phospho-signal that arises only after import collapse and that is confined to insoluble TDP-43 fractions would place phosphorylation downstream of the transport defect.

Limitations. Kinase identity, site identity, and phospho-mimetic fidelity are assumptions; the mitotic analogy is unverified prior knowledge, not a result from [S1]–[S4]. Phosphorylation of additional nucleoporins could make a two-nucleoporin edit uninformative. iPSC motor neurons model aging only in part. Stress-granule blockade and kinase inhibition have actions beyond the pore. A result in TDP-43 models would leave C9orf72 ALS and other sporadic trajectories unresolved. The proposal is an experimental hypothesis, not a demonstrated mechanism or a therapeutic claim.

## Public critiques

grok-r0
- The inducible cytoplasmic-biased TDP-43 model forces the localization change the hypothesis treats as downstream, so NPC phosphorylation cannot be ordered before nuclear loss of that species. Start from a perturbation that still permits TDP-43 import, and time NPC-enriched phospho-Nup against endogenous nuclear-versus-cytoplasmic TDP-43.
- The initiating kinase and phospho-acceptor residues stay unnamed, and a negative phospho-mimetic is written as falsifying despite the stated mimicry limit. Pre-register one kinase and separate Nup98 versus Nup62 site clusters, and count a mimetic-negative result as falsifying only after the mimic is shown to weaken FG cohesion or barrier selectivity.
- Nup98 and Nup62 are edited and interpreted together, so a hit cannot assign which nucleoporin is causal and a miss leaves other nucleoporins open. Run single-nucleoporin allele tests with a pre-specified rule for promoting any different NPC phospho-species found by mass spectrometry.

grok-r1
- The initiating event stays at class level: a provisional pair (Nup98 and Nup62), an unnamed proline-directed kinase, and “analogous” phospho-acceptor sites. Name one assumed kinase plus one or two alternates, and either a minimal residue set or a two-stage plan in which mass spectrometry defines sites before endogenous edits and site-specific reagents are used.
- Inducible cytoplasmic-biased TDP-43 already imposes the localization phenotype the hypothesis says phosphorylation causes, so timing in that system cannot show that pore phosphorylation initiates import failure. Run the initiation test in neurons that begin with nuclear wild-type TDP-43 under a defined stimulus, and use cytoplasmic-biased TDP-43 only for the aggregation-first alternative.
- Weakened FG cohesion is imported from an extra-packet mitotic analogy and is not measured apart from scaffold loss or passive leak. Add a selectivity panel—facilitated NLS/NES reporters versus size-restricted inert probes, plus a scaffold nucleoporin control—so barrier failure can be falsified even if Nup98/Nup62 phospho-signals change.

grok-r2
- The initiating enzyme is only the class “stress-activated proline-directed kinase,” so the early-versus-late inhibition arm has no gene, inhibitor, or knockdown to run. Name one candidate kinase as an explicit assumption, or add a kinase-identification step (phospho-motif mapping plus a preset inhibitor or knockdown panel) before that timed test.
- Nup98 and Nup62 are named, but the phospho-acceptor residues are only “analogous,” while the protocol already requires site-specific detection and endogenous phospho-dead and phospho-mimetic alleles. Map NPC-enriched sites by targeted mass spectrometry first, edit only sites that rise before import failure, and state the falsifier at residue resolution.
- The mitotic bridge treats FG-nucleoporin phosphorylation as slowing facilitated import while the pore scaffold stays in place; mitotic phosphorylation is principally an NPC-disassembly signal, and that gap is carrying the causal claim. Mark scaffold persistence as its own speculative bridge and add a contemporaneous scaffold readout (Nup107 or Nup133 localization and pore number). Rephrase “co-aggregation of nucleoporins” to S1’s supported claim of association with insoluble TDP-43 and mislocalization, and attach premise PMIDs 29311743, 30853299, 29628143, and 26308891 to the ranked item while keeping the phosphorylation claim unsupported.
