# c003

Hypothesis. Phosphorylation of FG-nucleoporin Nup62 within assembled nuclear pores initiates selective import failure and nuclear TDP-43 loss in TDP-43 proteinopathy models of ALS.

The setting is neuronal TDP-43 proteinopathy, in which cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies and nuclear-pore abnormalities can be modeled together. C9orf72 repeat disease, SOD1-linked disease, and unselected sporadic ALS remain separate contexts, and a shared mechanism across them is an open question. The kinase, the residues, and the choice of Nup62 are assumptions. Unverified prior knowledge only motivates that choice: Nup62 is a central-channel FG-nucleoporin, and FG-nucleoporin phosphorylation can accompany pore remodeling in other cellular states. That background lies outside [S1]–[S4] and carries no citation identifier.

Supported premises come from the packet. Nuclear-pore and transport components associate with insoluble TDP-43, disease-linked TDP-43 disrupts nucleoporin localization and nuclear transport in cells, and related pore pathology occurs in ALS tissue [S1]. Expanded C9orf72 RNA is linked to RanGAP mislocalization and impaired nuclear import, and transport or repeat-RNA manipulations can modify those model phenotypes, defining a genetic-context-specific route [S2]. Cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies can form without conventional stress granules, mislocalize transport-associated proteins, and accompany import failure, nuclear TDP-43 loss, and cell death; phosphorylated TDP-43 accumulates in those assemblies, documenting TDP-43 phosphorylation rather than nucleoporin phosphorylation [S3]. Cellular stress can recruit transport factors into stress granules and impair transport, and changing stress-granule assembly can modify those defects [S4]. The packet therefore separates intrinsic pore alteration [S1], stress-granule sequestration [S4], stress-granule-independent TDP-43 assemblies [S3], and a C9orf72 RanGAP route [S2]. It supplies no nucleoporin phosphorylation event as an initiating cause.

Speculative bridges supply the missing step. Once cytoplasmic TDP-43 de-mixing has begun, an assumed proline-directed kinase, ERK1/2 or a cyclin-dependent kinase, phosphorylates a pre-specified cluster of FG-region serine and threonine residues on Nup62 molecules that remain inside assembled pores. The assumed biophysical effect is weaker FG-repeat cohesion and slower facilitated import of nuclear-localization-signal cargo, including TDP-43. Under this bridge, Nup62 phosphorylation is the initiating pore-intrinsic mechanism. Nuclear TDP-43 depletion, further cytoplasmic accumulation, and neuronal dysfunction are downstream responses. Phosphorylated TDP-43 within assemblies [S3] is treated as a marker of those assemblies, parallel to the pore lesion under test.

Alternative explanations remain fully viable. Stress granules may sequester transport factors and thereby impair transport [S4], with nucleoporin phosphorylation only a coincident stress-kinase byproduct. Stress-granule-independent TDP-43 assemblies may capture transport factors and nucleoporins [S1, S3], so mislocalization would account for the phenotype with phosphorylation downstream or absent. C9orf72 repeat RNA and RanGAP mislocalization may initiate import failure on their own [S2]. Broader Ran-gradient or energetic failure may impair transport after neuronal injury is already underway, again placing any Nup62 phosphorylation downstream of dysfunction.

The discriminating preclinical test uses human iPSC-derived motor neurons in three arms: cytoplasmic TDP-43 de-mixing with a low stress-granule burden, following [S3]; acute stress-granule induction, following [S4]; and C9orf72 repeat-RNA expression, following [S2]. One time series measures Nup62 phosphorylation stoichiometry at the pre-specified sites in NPC-enriched fractions, NLS-reporter import rate, the nuclear-to-cytoplasmic TDP-43 ratio, G3BP1 stress-granule area, cytoplasmic TDP-43 assembly burden, RanGAP localization, and a delayed death marker. Endogenous Nup62 is replaced by phospho-deficient serine/threonine-to-alanine or phospho-mimetic serine/threonine-to-aspartate variants at those same sites. An orthogonal arm combines kinase knockdown with a second, chemically distinct inhibitor of the assumed kinase. Support would consist of preserved NLS import and nuclear TDP-43 with phospho-deficient Nup62 in the de-mixing arm while assemblies and any stress granules remain, slowed import with phospho-mimetic Nup62 before assemblies form, and weaker dependence on these Nup62 sites in the C9orf72 arm than in the TDP-43 de-mixing arm. Rescue that appears only when stress granules or TDP-43 assemblies disperse would favor sequestration [S3, S4]. A requirement limited to the repeat-RNA arm would favor the C9orf72 route [S2]. Temporal order is part of the discrimination: phosphorylation must rise before import declines and before the death marker if it is to count as initiating rather than as a downstream correlate of injury.

Falsifying observation. In the TDP-43 de-mixing arm the hypothesis is false if Nup62 phospho-stoichiometry at the pre-specified sites stays flat while NLS import and nuclear TDP-43 fall; if phospho-deficient Nup62 leaves that import failure and nuclear TDP-43 loss intact while assemblies persist; and if phospho-mimetic Nup62 leaves import intact in assembly-free neurons. Unchanged Nup62 phospho-occupancy together with quantitative sequestration of transport factors would favor the sequestration accounts [S3, S4].

Limitations. Kinase identity, site identity, and the focus on Nup62 are assumptions, and the unverified FG-nucleoporin background is not packet evidence. Phospho-mimetic substitutions only approximate a phosphate. Redundant phosphorylation of other nucleoporins could hide or mimic a single-site effect. iPSC motor neurons omit aging, glia, and circuit context, so a positive result would remain a hypothesis about one TDP-43 setting. Allele replacement is the decisive perturbation because kinase inhibitors have off-target actions. The design tests a research hypothesis and does not validate a treatment.

## Public critiques

grok-r0
- The kinase and the FG-region sites stay an open disjunction (ERK1/2 or a cyclin-dependent kinase; an unnamed pre-specified cluster), so the allele swap and the inhibitor arm have no fixed molecular target. Name one kinase, list the serine/threonine residues, and define the NPC-fraction stoichiometry assay before treating those perturbations as a falsifier.
- The headline calls Nup62 phosphorylation the initiating event, while the written chain begins only after cytoplasmic TDP-43 de-mixing is already underway. Restate initiation as relative to NLS-import failure and nuclear TDP-43 loss, and label de-mixing as the upstream condition the pore lesion is supposed to follow.
- S1 reports nucleoporin mislocalization with TDP-43 pathology, which strains the claim that the relevant Nup62 remains inside assembled pores, and the support criteria use unquantified words such as preserved, slowed, and weaker dependence. Co-measure Nup62 occupancy in the same NPC fractions as phospho-stoichiometry, add a non-NLS permeability control, and set numeric cutoffs and time-order rules that separate a pore-intrinsic effect from sequestration.

grok-r1
- The molecular target is not locked: the kinase is “ERK1/2 or a cyclin-dependent kinase” and the modified sites are only an unnamed “pre-specified cluster,” so the claim cannot be synthesized or inhibited as written. Freeze one kinase and the exact Nup62 FG-region serine/threonine positions, with that choice fixed before any outcome data.
- Falsification is phrased as one compound condition (“if A; if B; and if C”), which merges separate tests of temporal precedence, necessity, and sufficiency. State each result as its own falsifier and say which single outcome rejects the initiating-pore claim.
- The causal chain and the support criteria disagree: the bridge starts only after cytoplasmic TDP-43 de-mixing, yet predicted support includes phospho-mimetic import failure before assemblies form. Declare whether Nup62 phosphorylation is only a mediator downstream of de-mixing or is also sufficient in assembly-free neurons, and align the arm analysis with that claim.

grok-r2
- The FG-region cluster is called pre-specified yet no residue numbers or sequence context are given, so the stoichiometry assay and allele swap cannot be run as written; lock an explicitly assumed site list, or insert a site-mapping step whose output is frozen before the replacement experiment.
- ERK1/2 or a cyclin-dependent kinase is a disjunction, so one knockdown-plus-inhibitor arm does not test a single initiating kinase; commit to one kinase as the assumption under test, or pre-declare separate arms with separate falsifiers.
- Support and falsification lack effect-size cutoffs, a timing window, and a non-NLS cargo that would define selective import, and the three falsifying observations are joined so it is unclear whether each is independently sufficient; state each reject condition as independently decisive and predefine the spared-cargo control plus quantitative thresholds.
