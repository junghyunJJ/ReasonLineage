# Unregistered public response

Not registered as a candidate because output validation or the writing batch failed.

Hypothesis. ERK2 phosphorylation of pore-resident FG-nucleoporin Nup62 at Ser59, Ser84, Ser178, and Ser221 initiates selective NLS-import failure and nuclear TDP-43 loss downstream of cytoplasmic TDP-43 de-mixing in TDP-43 proteinopathy models of ALS.

The setting is neuronal TDP-43 proteinopathy. C9orf72 repeat disease is a comparison arm. SOD1-linked disease and unselected sporadic ALS stay outside this claim. Cytoplasmic TDP-43 de-mixing is the upstream condition. Phosphorylation at these sites is the pore-intrinsic step that initiates selective NLS-import failure and nuclear TDP-43 loss. Neuronal death is a later response. ERK2 (MAPK1) is the single assumed kinase. Ser59, Ser84, Ser178, and Ser221 are the stipulated site list, fixed before outcome data. Unverified prior knowledge only motivates an FG-region focus and the use of scaffold nucleoporin Nup107 as an occupancy normalizer. That background lies outside [S1]–[S4] and carries no citation identifier.

Supported premises come from the packet. Nuclear-pore and transport components associate with insoluble TDP-43, disease-linked TDP-43 disrupts nucleoporin localization and nuclear transport in cells, and related pore pathology occurs in ALS tissue [S1]. Expanded C9orf72 RNA is linked to RanGAP mislocalization and impaired nuclear import, and transport or repeat-RNA manipulations can modify those model phenotypes [S2]. Cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies can form without conventional stress granules, mislocalize transport-associated proteins, and accompany import failure, nuclear TDP-43 loss, and cell death; phosphorylated TDP-43 in those assemblies documents TDP-43 phosphorylation [S3]. Cellular stress can recruit transport factors into stress granules and impair transport, and changing stress-granule assembly can modify those defects [S4]. The packet supplies no nucleoporin phosphorylation event as a cause.

Speculative bridges. After de-mixing has begun, assumed ERK2 phosphorylates pore-resident Nup62 at the four stipulated sites. The assumed effect is weaker FG-repeat cohesion and slower facilitated import of NLS cargo, including TDP-43, while a size-matched non-NLS reporter keeps its nuclear-entry rate. Nuclear TDP-43 depletion follows that import defect. Because [S1] documents nucleoporin mislocalization, the bridge applies only while Nup62 remains in the NPC fraction.

Alternatives remain fully viable. Stress granules may sequester transport factors and impair transport [S4]. Stress-granule-independent assemblies may capture transport factors and nucleoporins [S1, S3]. C9orf72 repeat RNA and RanGAP mislocalization may drive import failure on their own [S2]. Broader Ran-gradient or energetic failure may impair transport after injury is underway.

Discriminating test. Human iPSC-derived motor neurons are studied in three arms: cytoplasmic TDP-43 de-mixing with a low stress-granule burden [S3]; acute stress-granule induction [S4]; and C9orf72 repeat-RNA expression [S2]. The claim is mediator status after de-mixing. An assembly-free phospho-mimetic result is uninformative for this claim. Phospho-mimetic Nup62 is used only in the de-mixing arm, as an augmentation probe under that upstream condition. Time points are 0, 2, 4, 8, 12, and 24 h. Endpoints are mean four-site phospho-stoichiometry, Nup62/Nup107 in the same fraction, NLS-reporter import rate, size-matched non-NLS reporter rate, nuclear-to-cytoplasmic TDP-43 ratio, G3BP1 area, cytoplasmic TDP-43 assembly burden, RanGAP localization, and a death marker. NPC fractions are nuclear envelopes from sequential detergent extraction. Stoichiometry is phospho/(phospho+unmodified) by parallel-reaction monitoring with stable-isotope AQUA peptides limited to Ser59, Ser84, Ser178, and Ser221. The prespecified endpoint is the mean of those four occupancies. Endogenous Nup62 is replaced by alanine or aspartate substitutions at only those residues. A corroborative arm combines ERK2 knockdown with a second, chemically distinct ERK2 inhibitor. Allele replacement is decisive.

Support thresholds in the de-mixing arm. Assembly burden reaches at least 2-fold at t_a. Mean phospho-occupancy then rises by at least 0.15 while Nup62/Nup107 remains at least 70% of baseline at t_p. At least 2 h later, NLS import falls by at least 40% and the non-NLS rate changes by less than 15%. The TDP-43 nuclear-to-cytoplasmic ratio then falls by at least 30%, and the death marker rises only afterward. Phospho-deficient Nup62 holds the NLS-import change under 15% and the TDP-43-ratio change under 15%, with assemblies still at least 2-fold, G3BP1 area within 20% of matched de-mixing cells, and Nup62/Nup107 at least 70%. Phospho-mimetic Nup62, still under de-mixing, deepens the NLS-import deficit by at least 25 percentage points. In the C9orf72 arm the alanine replacement restores at most 15 percentage points of the NLS deficit. Rescue confined to cultures whose G3BP1 area falls by at least 50% favors sequestration [S3, S4]. A requirement confined to the repeat-RNA arm favors the C9orf72 route [S2].

Falsifying observations. Each result rejects the initiating-pore claim on its own. The claim is rejected if NLS import falls by at least 40% and the TDP-43 ratio falls by at least 30% while mean four-site occupancy has risen by less than 0.15, including when Nup62/Nup107 remains at least 70%. The claim is rejected if phospho-deficient Nup62 leaves an NLS-import drop of at least 40% and a TDP-43-ratio drop of at least 30% intact, with assemblies still at least 2-fold and Nup62/Nup107 at least 70%. The selective-import claim is rejected if the non-NLS rate rises by at least 40% in the same window as the NLS drop. The pore-resident claim is rejected if Nup62/Nup107 falls below 70% of baseline before occupancy rises by 0.15 and import still fails.

Limitations. ERK2, the four residue numbers, the FG-region assignment, and the Nup107 normalizer are assumptions. Unverified background is not packet evidence. The AQUA panel stays restricted to those four residues for the life of the study; unmodified sites falsify this locked target. Phospho-mimetic side chains approximate phosphate and speak only under continued de-mixing. Phosphorylation of other nucleoporins could mask a single-protein effect. iPSC motor neurons omit aging, glia, and circuit context. ERK2 inhibitors have off-target actions. The design tests a research hypothesis and stops short of a treatment claim.
