Nup98 phosphorylation as a downstream amplifier of nuclear-import failure after cytoplasmic TDP-43 assembly in ALS/FTD-relevant neurons

The hypothesis is that CDK1 phosphorylation of NPC-retained Nup98 at human T529, S591, S822, and S861 amplifies nuclear-import failure after cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies form in ALS/FTD-relevant neurons. De-mixing of cytoplasmic TDP-43 is the initiating event. The four-site phosphate is a later modification of Nup98 that remains in assembled pores. On this hypothesis, that phosphate adds import failure beyond sequestration, broad stress, and Nup98 exit.

Insoluble TDP-43 aggregates associate with nuclear-pore and transport components. Disease-linked TDP-43 disrupts nucleoporin localization and transport in experimental cells, and related pore pathology is present in ALS tissue [S1]. Nucleoporin mislocalization in [S1] is distinct from a gating phosphate on pores that retain Nup98, and [S1] names neither a phospho-site nor one sequence for every ALS subtype. Cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies can form without conventional stress granules, mislocalize transport proteins, and accompany import failure, nuclear TDP-43 loss, and cell death; phosphorylated TDP-43 in those assemblies is a TDP-43 modification [S3]. C9orf72 repeat RNA is a separate RanGAP-mislocalization route to import failure [S2]. Stress recruits transport factors into stress granules and impairs transport, and changing stress-granule assembly modifies that phenotype [S4]. With [S3], stress granules are dispensable for some TDP-43-linked defects.

Unverified prior knowledge: Nup98 is a central-channel FG-nucleoporin, and mitotic CDK1 phosphorylates T529, S591, S822, and S861 as the envelope breaks down. The bridge is that after assemblies form in post-mitotic neurons, CDK1 writes this four-site mark on NPC-incorporated Nup98 and thereby slows facilitated import enough to worsen TDP-43 re-entry. [S1]-[S4] name neither kinase, residues, nor order. Mass spectrometry of isolated NPCs must show these sites occupied on pore-retained Nup98. If isolated NPCs instead show a different occupied set, the alanine allele is rebuilt to those detected residues before rescue is interpreted. The causal reagent is Nup98 with alanine at exactly those four residues; CDK1 inhibitors stay off that arm because CDK1 has many substrates. Further unverified prior knowledge: FG-nucleoporins exchange over hours to days, whereas neuronal scaffold nucleoporins can last months.

Alternatives can yield the same transport failure. Sequestration in stress granules [S4] or TDP-43 assemblies [S3] can impair import without a required Nup98 phosphate. RanGAP mislocalization can explain import failure in C9orf72-expansion carriers [S2]. Phospho-Nup98 may only report stress-kinase activity, and the mitotic precedent allows phosphate to release Nup98 from the pore, the localization change in [S1].

The test uses human iPSC-derived motor neurons without a C9orf72 expansion, so [S2] is outside the background. Light onset at t = 0 on optoTDP-43 neurons is the trigger; the assumed construct retains an NLS and is meant to form the granule-independent cytoplasmic assemblies in [S3]. Tagged wild-type or four-site alanine Nup98 is expressed from day -10. Assays at 6 h, 24 h, 72 h, and 7 days record assemblies, stress-granule markers, nuclear TDP-43, death, Nup98 rim intensity, NLS-reporter import rate and nucleus/cytoplasm ratio, the tagged fraction of NPC-bound Nup98, and four-site phospho-stoichiometry on isolated NPCs together with rim intensity.

Predicted order is assemblies plus a partial import deficit while four-site NPC occupancy remains below 10% of later stoichiometry (pre-phosphate window, expected 6 h to 24 h), then a further import drop from 72 h through day 7 with rim intensity preserved. Incremental worsening is early minus late import rate in assembly-positive cells. Rescue scoring requires tagged alanine-Nup98 to be at least 50% of NPC-bound Nup98 at 72 h and day 7. Success is recovery of at least half that increment, assemblies within 20% of tag-wild-type pathology, rim intensity within 15% of that control, and at least a 70% drop in four-site NPC stoichiometry. A G3BP-dependent stress-granule block tests [S4]; persistence of NPC phospho-Nup98 and the increment without stress granules aligns the mark with [S3]. Phospho-mimetic Nup98 at the same four sites, without optoTDP-43 light, is a 24 h autonomy test: slower import with retained rim Nup98 and little new insoluble TDP-43 fits an amplifier; rim loss means disassembly; a rapid aggregation wave means an initiator.

Falsifying observations are unoccupied four sites on isolated NPCs while import fails, nuclear TDP-43 falls, and neurons die; phosphate restricted to granule-associated transport factors; alanine-Nup98 at or above 50% NPC incorporation leaving the incremental worsening unchanged while assemblies persist; import scaling with rim loss and no added contribution from stoichiometry on remaining Nup98; or the four-site mark preceding TDP-43 redistribution, including launch of assembly and death by phospho-mimetic Nup98 alone.

Limitations. CDK1, the four residues, optoTDP-43, G3BP dependence, and FG-exchange timing are unverified prior knowledge outside [S1]-[S4]. Phospho-mimetics only approximate phosphate. iPSC motor neurons omit long human aging and do not represent every ALS subtype. Other FG-nucleoporins may compensate, and a misassigned peptide could imitate an NPC signal. The plan is unvalidated.
