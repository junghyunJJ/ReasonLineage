# Pd3177a39af

HYPOTHESIS TITLE: Stress-triggered phosphorylation of Nup98 at mitotic-disassembly-linked GLFG-repeat S/T-P sites initiates nucleocytoplasmic transport failure upstream of TDP-43 mislocalization in TDP-43-proteinopathy ALS motor neurons.

MECHANISM AND CAUSAL CHAIN. The proposed initiating event is a pore-intrinsic post-translational modification in post-mitotic motor neurons: ALS-relevant stress activates a proline-directed kinase that phosphorylates Nup98 at GLFG-domain S/T-P serine/threonine motifs — the same site class that is phosphorylated to drive partial NPC disassembly during mitosis. Mitotic Nup98 phosphorylation is documented in prior literature (recalled from training data, not supplied by the packet); it is therefore reclassified from a speculative bridge to a known biological premise about mitosis, with published mitotic phospho-proteomic maps supplying residue-level annotation from which candidate GLFG-repeat serines should be selected (not invented here). The remaining speculative causal chain is: phosphorylated Nup98 weakens the FG-permeability barrier without wholesale disassembly; the Ran gradient and classical NLS-import collapse locally; nuclear TDP-43 is lost; cytoplasmic TDP-43 de-mixes and acquires pathological phosphorylation within persistent assemblies [S3]; those assemblies and stress granules sequester transport factors, amplifying the defect [S3, S4]. Nucleoporin phosphorylation initiates; sequestration is a downstream amplifier; TDP-43 phosphorylation is a consequence.

SUPPORTED PREMISES FROM THE PACKET. (1) TDP-43 pathology associates with nuclear-pore/transport components and disrupts nucleoporin localization and transport in experimental systems and ALS tissue [S1]. (2) C9orf72 repeat expansion causes RanGAP mislocalization and impaired import in models and patient-derived neurons [S2]. (3) Persistent cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies impair import, deplete nuclear TDP-43, and drive cell death independently of conventional stress granules [S3]. (4) Stress granules sequester transport factors and impair transport; perturbing granule assembly modifies phenotypes [S4]. None establishes nucleoporin phosphorylation as causal.

PRIOR-KNOWLEDGE PREMISE AND REMAINING SPECULATIVE BRIDGES. Documented prior-literature premise (not packet): mitotic Nup98 phosphorylation by CDK1-like activity occurs during NPC disassembly. Bridge 1 (now narrowed): interphase ALS-relevant stress ectopically re-phosphorylates those mitotic-type GLFG sites in post-mitotic neurons — no packet or packet-external evidence establishes this for ALS. Bridge 2: the interphase kinase is individually testable; explicit candidates are CDK5 (proline-directed, highly expressed in post-mitotic neurons) and stress-activated ERK/MAPK, both capable of S/T-P phosphorylation (CDK5 and MAPK neuronal roles are unverified prior knowledge; their activation under ALS stress is an assumption). Bridge 3: phosphorylation temporally precedes import collapse.

ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS. (A) Sequestration-only: transport factors trapped in granules [S4] or TDP-43 assemblies [S3] cause failure; phosphorylation is a secondary stress response. (B) Direct-binding: insoluble TDP-43 mechanically disrupts pore components; phosphorylation incidental [S1]. (C) C9orf72-specific repeat-RNA or dipeptide mechanisms impair transport without pore modification [S2].

DISCRIMINATING PRECLINICAL TEST STRATEGY. System: iPSC-derived motor neurons (C9orf72 carrier and TDP-43-de-mixing induction) with live NLS-import reporter and pre-stress baseline measurements of import rate, mRNA export, FG-barrier function, and pore ultrastructure.
1. Site nomination and reagent validation. Enumerate Nup98 GLFG S/T-P serines against published mitotic phospho-site maps; generate phospho-antibodies to top candidates; validate specificity by (i) signal loss in phospho-dead knock-in, (ii) signal gain in phospho-mimetic knock-in, (iii) LC-MS/MS confirmation. Pre-register detection limit and occupancy threshold (e.g., ≥5% occupancy by MS or phospho-signal ≥3σ above phospho-dead background).
2. Temporal precedence (primary discriminator). Sampling every 5 minutes from stress onset for ≥6 hours, quantifying phospho-Nup98, import rate, and granule burden at each interval. Pre-registered rule: the pore-intrinsic model requires phospho-Nup98 detectable at or before the first time point at which import declines ≥15% from baseline, and before or at earliest detectable granule nucleation.
3. Necessity with baseline controls. Introduce single-site (not multi-site) phospho-dead edits at individually nominated GLFG serines, including at least one kinase-docking-disruptive edit. Before stress induction, require baseline import, mRNA export, and pore function indistinguishable from wild-type; clones with perturbed baselines are excluded as uninterpretable.
4. Sufficiency and directionality. Inducible phospho-mimetic Nup98 should reproduce import decline and trigger TDP-43 mislocalization without cytoplasmic assemblies; early NPC-targeted phosphatase recruitment should partially rescue under stress, late recruitment should not.
5. Sequestration discrimination — revised. Temporal precedence (step 2), not pharmacological granule blockade, is the primary discriminator, because ISRIB or G3BP1/2 deletion may blunt upstream stress rather than only removing granules. Orthogonal granule-disruption approaches are secondary; residual granule burden is quantified in every arm, and arms with persistent residual granules are weighted as unable to cleanly separate models.
6. In vivo specification with quantitative rules. Contexts matching hypothesis scope: (a) TDP-43 proteinopathy — rNLS8 or equivalent inducible TDP-43 ΔNLS line (not SOD1); (b) C9orf72 repeat-expansion mice. AAV9 hSyn or ChAT promoter at pre-defined dose and delivery age; pre-symptomatic sampling (e.g., P30, P60, P90). Decision rule: within the same animal, phospho-Nup98 must be detectable ≥1 sampling interval before RanGAP mislocalization or import decline.

FALSIFYING OBSERVATION. If, at the pre-registered temporal resolution and occupancy thresholds, phospho-Nup98 first appears after or simultaneously with import collapse in both contexts, and single-site phospho-dead knock-ins with verified-unperturbed baseline show identical timing and magnitude of import failure, RanGAP mislocalization, and nuclear TDP-43 depletion as wild-type, the hypothesis is falsified and Nup98 phosphorylation is recast as a downstream stress response supporting sequestration or direct-binding alternatives [S1, S3, S4].

LIMITATIONS. The packet contains no direct evidence that nucleoporin phosphorylation initiates ALS; Bridges 1–3 remain assumptions. Mitotic Nup98 phosphorylation is documented prior literature, not packet evidence. The scope is restricted to TDP-43-proteinopathy contexts (C9orf72 and TDP-43-mislocalizing sporadic disease), not all ALS. Nup98 is dosage-sensitive; single-site edits retain baseline-perturbation risk, addressed but not eliminated by step 3. Phospho-antibody specificity for low-abundance neuronal targets is a known pitfall addressed by knock-in gain/loss-of-signal validation. Results must be interpreted perturbationally, not correlationally.
