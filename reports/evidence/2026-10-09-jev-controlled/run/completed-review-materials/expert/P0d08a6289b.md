# P0d08a6289b

Hypothesis title: Phosphorylation of NUP98 initiates nuclear-import failure in TDP-43 proteinopathy models of ALS.

This proposal tests one prespecified claim: in experimental TDP-43 proteinopathy, phosphorylation of the FG-nucleoporin NUP98 is an initiating nuclear-pore alteration that impairs selective nuclear import and thereby promotes nuclear TDP-43 depletion and neuronal dysfunction. The initiating event is covalent modification of NUP98. Nuclear TDP-43 loss, cytoplasmic TDP-43 assembly, and cell death are downstream responses that may also proceed in parallel. The claim is limited to TDP-43 proteinopathy models and TDP-43-positive ALS tissue. NUP62 and NUP153 stay separate hypotheses.

Supported premises. Disease-linked TDP-43 associates with insoluble nuclear-pore and transport components, disrupts nucleoporin localization and nuclear transport in cell systems, and accompanies pore pathology in ALS tissue [S1]. This supports causal study of pore alteration while leaving the initiating chemistry unspecified [S1]. Persistent cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies can form independently of conventional stress granules, mislocalize transport-associated proteins, and coincide with impaired import, nuclear TDP-43 loss, and cell death [S3]. Phosphorylated TDP-43 in those assemblies is a TDP-43 modification, separate from nucleoporin phosphorylation [S3]. Expanded C9orf72 RNA is linked to RanGAP mislocalization and impaired import, and transport-directed manipulations can modify phenotypes in that genetic context [S2]. Cellular stress recruits transport factors into stress granules and impairs transport; altering stress-granule assembly can modify those defects [S4].

Speculative bridges. NUP98, the kinase, and the acceptor sites are assumptions. Unverified prior knowledge holds that NUP98 FG repeats contribute to the selective barrier and that mitotic CDK1 phosphorylates NUP98 at proline-directed sites during nuclear-envelope breakdown, loosening that barrier. The [S1] paraphrase supports nucleoporin mislocalization and does not name NUP98. The assumed bridge is that a proline-directed kinase, provisionally a CDK-family enzyme, places a partial analogous mark on NUP98 in post-mitotic neurons during TDP-43 proteinopathy. The prespecified neuronal effect is dual: FG-barrier compromise slows importin-mediated NLS import and raises passive permeability of an inert probe, while the Ran gradient and ATP stay intact at onset. Speculatively, mislocalization in [S1] may be phospho-dependent release of NUP98. Mitotic envelope breakdown is an analogy for barrier chemistry. Kinase action on NUP98 in stressed neurons remains an assumption until measured in the same model.

Alternative explanations. Stress granules may sequester transport factors so import fails while the pore stays covalently intact [S4]. Cytoplasmic TDP-43 de-mixing may inhibit import directly and place pore changes downstream [S3]. Aggregates may displace nucleoporins, leaving later phosphorylation as a mark on damaged pores [S1]. C9orf72 repeat RNA and RanGAP mislocalization may explain import failure in that genetic context [S2]. Ran-gradient collapse or energetic failure can slow import with unmodified NUP98.

Discriminating preclinical test. Human iPSC-derived motor neurons with inducible disease-linked TDP-43, plus a matched stress paradigm that assembles stress granules in the absence of mutant TDP-43, are sampled densely around import onset. An enabling observation precedes any allele program in that exact model: proline-directed kinase activity by p-Rb and MPM2 reactivity, and NUP98 phospho-peptide abundance by deep phospho-enrichment plus targeted mass spectrometry, with site-specific antibody confirmation when reagents exist. Sites that rise on NPC-incorporated NUP98 before the import-kinetic decline, and the kinase whose activity rises in that same window, are nominated and locked before confirmatory experiments.

The locked pattern at onset is slower NLS-reporter import, higher passive permeability of a size-matched inert probe, and a preserved Ran nuclear-to-cytoplasmic gradient and cellular ATP. A Ran/ATP-controlled permeabilized import assay then supplies defined Ran, importin-β, and an ATP/GTP regenerating system to nuclei from the same cells. Persistence of the import defect after reconstitution supports an intrinsic phosphorylated-pore lesion. Restoration by those exogenous factors supports transport-factor shortage or energetic failure. Parallel readouts track G3BP1-positive stress granules, stress-granule-independent cytoplasmic TDP-43 assemblies, importin and RanGAP redistribution, and the nuclear-to-cytoplasmic TDP-43 ratio. Dense enrichment and targeted mass spectrometry reduce the chance of missing a sub-stoichiometric or transient mark.

Confirmatory alleles use endogenous editing of the locked NUP98 sites, or validated replacement that removes phosphorylatable endogenous NUP98. Targeted mass spectrometry must show loss of those phospho-peptides on NPC-incorporated NUP98, with normal baseline import and permeability, before any phenotype is interpreted. Prevention edits sites before phospho-onset; late rescue begins only after import has failed. A CDK-family inhibitor and genetic loss of the nominated kinase are read only together with the verified site edits, because CDKs modify many substrates. A crossed arm blocks stress-granule assembly while phospho-status, permeability, and import kinetics are measured independently. The same assay in C9orf72-repeat neurons tests whether a RanGAP-linked import defect [S2] requires the locked NUP98 mark.

Falsifying observation. After verified loss of the locked phospho-peptides on NPC-incorporated NUP98 and normal baseline transport, the initiating-site claim is falsified if pre-onset blockade leaves import-kinetic failure, the passive-permeability increase, and nuclear TDP-43 loss unchanged in timing and severity, or if the mark appears only after import has failed or only with insoluble TDP-43. Full restoration of NLS import by exogenous Ran, importin-β, and ATP/GTP despite the mark falsifies an intrinsic pore-chemistry cause. A phosphomimetic that leaves import and permeability intact weakens sufficiency. Failed late rescue after assemblies are present leaves initiation intact, because parallel downstream injury can persist. Import restored by stress-granule blockade despite the mark and altered permeability favors sequestration [S4].

Limitations. Kinase identity and sites lie outside [S1]–[S4] until the enabling observation assigns them. p-Rb and MPM2 report proline-directed activity and leave NUP98 substrate identity open until peptides are mapped. Phosphomimetics approximate phosphate. CDK perturbation is broader than one site. iPSC-neuron timing may differ from human disease chronology. Extremely brief marks can still be missed. The design tests a research hypothesis and does not validate a treatment.
