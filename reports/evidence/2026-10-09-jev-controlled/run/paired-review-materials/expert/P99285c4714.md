# P99285c4714

Title: Conditionally repurposing bedaquiline, an approved antitubercular agent, to collapse oxidative phosphorylation in cytarabine-resistant, oxidative-metabolism-dependent residual AML — gated on verified human mitochondrial ATP-synthase inhibition and achievable free-drug exposure

Hypothesis (stated as a condition, not a premise). IF, at clinically achievable unbound concentrations, bedaquiline directly inhibits mammalian mitochondrial F1Fo-ATP synthase in AML cells, THEN it will deplete ATP at the terminal oxidative-phosphorylation (OXPHOS) step and preferentially eliminate the post-cytarabine residual, oxidative-metabolism-dependent disease state — cells not enriched for leukemia stem cells (LSCs), yet dependent on oxidative metabolism with fatty-acid-oxidation features [S2] — with co-coverage of the ROS-low, BCL-2-high quiescent LSC fraction [S1] tracked as a separate endpoint. Human complex-V inhibition is deliberately carried as a conditional test rather than a fact: it is unverified prior knowledge, verified first, before any AML work. Bedaquiline's original indication is multidrug-resistant tuberculosis, but every drug-level fact (approval, protein binding, half-life, QT prolongation, human complex-V potency) is unverified prior knowledge requiring regulatory and pharmacology confirmation. This is a direct-inhibition analogue of the venetoclax-azacitidine metabolic-disruption comparator [S3]; reproducing that regimen is not the proposal.

Supported premises (within packet limits). (1) An OXPHOS-linked, ROS-low, BCL-2-high LSC subset exists, and OXPHOS reduction affected quiescent LSCs; within the packet this dependency is demonstrated only for the tested BCL-2 inhibitor, and the BCL-2-OXPHOS relationship there is an association, not a proven causal ordering [S1]. (2) Cytarabine-resistant residual cells are not necessarily LSCs but require oxidative metabolism; altering that state changed response [S2], mandating separate bulk-versus-LSC endpoints. (3) Disrupting LSC energy metabolism is clinically attainable, as shown for the comparator combination [S3]. (4) Metabolic inhibition can promote differentiation rather than killing, so myeloid differentiation is monitored as an outcome channel [S4].

Speculative bridges, each with a pre-committed test. (a) Target bridge (assumption): recalled prior knowledge attributes bedaquiline's antitubercular effect to mycobacterial F-ATP synthase, and preclinical literature reportedly claims low-micromolar inhibition of human complex V; external input also suggests bedaquiline may already have been explored in oncology as a mammalian ATP-synthase agent. All of this is unverified and becomes the gating experiment. (b) Exposure bridge with a quantitative gate (assumption): regulatory PK at the approved dose (Cmax, free fraction; to be verified) will be converted to a projected unbound range, which must approach the measured intact-cell IC50 for oligomycin-sensitive respiration; margin will be allowed only for quantified intracellular/tissue accumulation, since extreme protein binding and slow clearance make this the weakest link. (c) Dependency bridge: residual oxidative cells may lack glycolytic reserve; tested directly by methyl-pyruvate/glycolytic rescue with ECAR/OCR in the actual residual population. (d) LSC bridge: direct complex-V inhibition is an extrapolation beyond the only OXPHOS dependency supported in the packet (BCL-2 inhibition, an association); explicitly speculative.

Prior-art delta. OXPHOS collapse as an AML strategy is derivative of S1-S3 and of tool-compound (oligomycin) logic. The only element claimed as novel is the drug-context pair: direct mammalian complex-V inhibition to eliminate post-cytarabine residual disease with LSC co-coverage. Targeted search criteria: (bedaquiline OR TMC207) crossed with (acute myeloid leukemia; ATP synthase/cancer stem cells; residual disease). Pre-committed consequences: confirmed prior bedaquiline-AML preclinical data reframes this as a residual-disease-context extension, not first-in-context; oncology complex-V data without AML residual data restricts novelty to the context and LSC co-coverage; if both exist, retention requires the discriminating residual-versus-LSC design below.

Alternative explanations. Killing could reflect cationic-amphiphile lysosomotropic stress, electron-transport backlog with ROS rather than ATP depletion, nonspecific lipophilic cytotoxicity, or differentiation without net killing [S4].

Discriminating preclinical strategy (ordered gates; failure halts progression). Gate 1, target: purified/recombinant human complex-V activity assay plus intact-cell IC50 for oligomycin-sensitive respiration, before any AML viability work. Gate 2, exposure: measured potency versus verified PK-derived unbound range for the approved dose; failure here falsifies the drug-level claim, not merely the context claim. Gate 3, mechanism and dependency in cytarabine-surviving residual populations from S2-type models and primary specimens stratified for ROS-low LSC content: galactose medium forcing OXPHOS reliance should sensitize; ATP5A1/ATP5B knockdown should phenocopy and occlude further drug effect; oligomycin should show concordant signatures; methyl-pyruvate rescue should reverse killing if ATP depletion is causal, and its failure in residual cells tests bridge (c). Gate 4, context: bedaquiline administered after, not before, cytarabine should shrink residual oxidative-metabolism disease, reduce LSC frequency in limiting-dilution assays, induce CD11b/CD14 differentiation [S4], and be benchmarked against venetoclax-azacitidine comparator cultures [S3]. Gate 5, window: paired normal CD34+ colony assays, cardiomyocyte/myotube ATP suppression, and a patient-derived xenograft residual-disease experiment with human-matched unbound exposure, serial ATP measurements, residual burden, LSC frequency, differentiation, and cardiac safety readouts, checking whether ATP depletion precedes death.

Falsifying observations (any single result blocks the drug-mechanism-context link). Cytotoxicity without suppression of oligomycin-sensitive or ATP-linked respiration (off-target mechanism); combined failure of galactose sensitization, knockdown phenocopy/occlusion, and oligomycin concordance (complex V not causal); methyl-pyruvate restoration of ATP and viability in residual cells (bridge c falsified); measured intact-cell IC50 exceeding the verified achievable unbound range (bridge b falsified at drug level); equal killing of glycolysis-reliant bulk cells and normal CD34+ progenitors at or below achievable free concentrations (no dependency or no therapeutic window); or residual-bulk shrinkage without LSC-frequency reduction (context claim falsified, consistent with the S2 caution).

Limitations. S1 and S2 are model-dependent and AML genotype heterogeneity is unaddressed. All bedaquiline-specific facts are unverified prior knowledge pending confirmation against regulatory and pharmacology sources. Direct complex-V inhibition plausibly threatens cardiac and skeletal muscle, and tissue accumulation complicates exposure modeling and combination scheduling. Confirmed prior bedaquiline-AML or oncology work changes novelty only through the pre-committed reframing above. This is a mechanistic hypothesis, not a treatment recommendation; established regimens remain standard of care.
