# Pdd66f09c00

Teriflunomide repurposing for uridine-rescuable differentiation of cytarabine-residual ROS-higher AML blasts

Hypothesis. In primary non-APL AML, teriflunomide at or below verified unbound Cmax will cause uridine-rescuable myeloid differentiation and lower leukemogenic output of cytarabine-residual blasts in a ROS-higher gate, only when that gate has higher baseline dihydroorotate-to-orotate flux than paired ROS-low cells. On-target DHODH inhibition is read out by reduced dihydroorotate-to-orotate flux and UDP/UTP depletion, and is scored apart from bulk kill, fatty-acid-oxidation blockade, and ROS-low stem-cell loss. Teriflunomide is treated as the active metabolite of leflunomide. Multiple-sclerosis approval, leflunomide approval for rheumatoid arthritis, and clinical DHODH targeting are unverified prior knowledge. Numeric free Cmax awaits verification.

Supported premises. Functionally defined leukemia stem cells in Lagadinou specimens frequently had low ROS and elevated BCL-2. BCL-2 inhibition reduced oxidative phosphorylation and affected quiescent leukemia stem cells, with the window for an arbitrary mitochondrial inhibitor left undefined [S1]. Farge cytarabine-residual cells were inconsistently enriched for immature quiescent leukemia stem cells, showed mitochondrial and fatty-acid-oxidation features, and changed response when that state was altered [S2]. Those residuals differ from the ROS-low population in [S1], and pyrimidine flux is unmeasured in [S2]. DHODH inhibition in Sykes models promoted myeloid differentiation and affected disease endpoints, while selectivity of each approved pathway drug stays unshown [S4]. Venetoclax with azacitidine is the established comparator for leukemia-stem-cell TCA-cycle and oxidative-phosphorylation effects [S3]. Repeating it lies outside this proposal.

Speculative bridges. The packet omits teriflunomide. Pharmacology assumes DHODH block at approved-indication unbound exposure. Oxidative and fatty-acid features [S2] stand apart from pyrimidine dependence. The proposal is that respiring ROS-higher residual blasts keep mitochondrial DHODH; ubiquinone topology is unverified prior knowledge. Stratum selectivity requires higher baseline dihydroorotate-to-orotate flux in residual ROS-higher cells than in paired ROS-low cells before any drug margin is counted, and uridine-to-UTP labeling stratifies salvage. The context claim is a larger uridine-rescuable drop in leukemia-initiating activity there than in ROS-low stem cells [S1] or treatment-naive bulk. Genotype is unspecified.

Alternative explanations. Marker gains may reflect stress or loss of immature cells. Ubiquinone sharing may collapse respiration in a venetoclax-like pattern [S1], [S3]. Fatty-acid-oxidation dependence may explain residual behavior and leave DHODH irrelevant [S2]. Normal progenitors may be equally sensitive. Marrow uridine may bypass DHODH in vivo.

Discriminating preclinical test. CellROX Deep Red, manufacturer viable-cell protocol, stains 7-AAD-negative CD45-dim blasts for 30 min at 37°C in serum-free medium, read unfixed, before any gate. Dye concordance with [S1] is unverified prior knowledge. ROS-low is the lowest quintile; ROS-higher is above the median (design choices). Cytarabine: 24 h at the sample concentration cutting bulk viability ≥70%, three washes, 48 h recovery, and continuation only if ≥2 million viable blasts remain (smaller yield = technical failure).

Stage 0, drug-free: stop unless residual ROS-higher dihydroorotate-to-orotate flux is ≥1.5× paired ROS-low flux, a design threshold. Median uridine-tracer labeling of UTP pre-declares high- and low-salvage halves. Stage 1 uses only residual ROS-higher cells, teriflunomide ≤ verified free Cmax, ± uridine at verified plasma concentration (~5 µmol/L unverified prior knowledge, replaced when checked). Go: dihydroorotate ≥2× vehicle, UDP+UTP ≤50% of vehicle, uridine restores both to within 20% of vehicle, CD11b+CD14+ rises ≥20 percentage points, and ≥30/100 blinded cells mature. n=12 primary non-APL specimens; paired Wilcoxon, two-sided α=0.05, within salvage half.

A nucleotide go precedes initiating-cell assays. Stage 2 adds strata, normal colonies, and arms ± plasma uridine: teriflunomide; brequinar matched on dihydroorotate within free Cmax (S4-class link unverified prior knowledge); venetoclax [S1], [S3]; etomoxir only if palmitate-supported OCR falls ≥50% and glucose-supported OCR stays within 20% of vehicle (CPT1 engagement unverified prior knowledge). A linear mixed model tests the treatment-by-stratum term on maturation and initiating-cell frequency with salvage half, α=0.05, in stage-0 passers only. Support is a ≥1.5× larger marker gain and initiating-cell drop in residual ROS-higher cells than in paired bulk and ROS-low cells, with CD34+ colonies ≥70% of vehicle. A xenograft of passers, unbound exposure ≤ human free Cmax, is falsified if marrow uridine holds blast UDP+UTP ≥80% of vehicle.

Falsifying observation. Falsifiers are absence of the stage-0 ≥1.5× flux excess; equal maturation by teriflunomide and brequinar in residual ROS-higher, ROS-low, and treatment-naive bulk cells [S4]; a treatment-by-stratum term under 1.5×; uridine restoration of UDP/UTP while differentiation and leukemogenic loss remain; an etomoxir or venetoclax copy without dihydroorotate accumulation and UDP/UTP depletion; concentrations above free Cmax or absent brequinar phenocopy within it; and CD34-positive colonies under 70% of vehicle. High-salvage-only misses are salvage bypass.

Limitations. [S1]–[S4] omit clinical efficacy and marrow selectivity for teriflunomide. Approval, protein binding, half-life, free Cmax, plasma uridine, brequinar potency, and etomoxir selectivity need external verification. Cytopenias and hepatotoxicity described for approved use are unverified prior knowledge and mark marrow and liver risk. n=12 is a yield lock and may underpower the interaction. Salvage, lineage bias, and genotype may restrict activity. Dose, schedule, and superiority claims are deferred.
