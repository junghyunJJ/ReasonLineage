Speculative bridges. Each candidate-specific step is speculative. In particular, phosphorylation might instead follow TDP-43 aggregation, represent an adaptive response, or merely mark broader cellular stress. Limited temporal resolution could obscure ordering, and immature cultured neurons may not reproduce aged motor neurons.
